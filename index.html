About the Unit

GHSS MUNDERI Rovers & Rangers aims to build leadership, discipline, and community service among students. We participate in school and community projects, environmental activities, and skill development programs.

  • Community service and cleanliness drives
  • First aid, safety, and life skills
  • Camps, hikes, and outdoor learning
  • Project-based learning and teamwork
